Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

What our games collect, why, and the choices you have — in plain English.

🚫 No accounts 📵 No data sold 📱 Progress stays on device

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use games published by Coding Ibex (“we”, “us”, “our”), how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies to our mobile game STAX (Google Play package com.codeibex.stax) and to any other app of ours that links to this policy.

The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account with us, and we do not collect your name, email address or payment details.
  • Your progress (high score, lives, settings, purchased skins) is stored on your own device.
  • Third-party services we use — advertising, Google Play Games and Google Play billing — may collect device and usage data. They are listed in full below.
  • On first launch we ask for your advertising consent, and you can change that choice at any time.
  • Want your data gone? See How to delete your data.

1. Who we are

Coding Ibex is an independent game studio. We are the “data controller” for the limited personal data processed through our games. You can reach us using the details in the Contact section.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us directly

None. Our games have no registration, no sign-up form and no contact form. We only receive personal information from you if you choose to email us.

2.2 Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device so it can remember your progress. This data stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Your high score and local score history
  • Your remaining lives and their refill timer
  • Settings such as sound and haptics (vibration) toggles
  • Which block skins you own and which one is equipped, and whether a subscription is active
  • Whether you have already seen the tutorial

2.3 Information collected by third-party services

Our games include third-party software (SDKs) that may collect information automatically. Broadly, these services may process:

  • Advertising identifiers — such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID)
  • Device and technical information — device model, operating system version, language, country, network/connection type, coarse location inferred from IP address, IP address, and app version
  • Advertising activity — ads shown to you, impressions, clicks and whether a rewarded ad was completed
  • Google Play Games profile data — if you sign in: your Play Games display name, player ID and the scores you submit to the leaderboard
  • Purchase information — a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase or subscription completed. Payment is handled entirely by Google; we never see or receive your card or bank details.

4. How the information is used

  • To run the game and remember your progress on your device
  • To display advertising, which keeps the game free to play
  • To show global leaderboards and your ranking, if you sign in to Google Play Games
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases and subscriptions
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems, and to keep the game working correctly

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Advertising and your consent

Ads may be personalised (based on an advertising profile) or non-personalised (based only on general context). Where required by law — for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom — the game shows a consent form on first launch, provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform, before any personalised ads are requested.

You can change your mind at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, where you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation.
  • Where a consent form is available in your region, you can revisit your choice from the in-game privacy option, if shown.

Opting out of personalised ads does not remove ads; it means the ads you see will be less relevant.

6. Google Play Games and leaderboards

Signing in to Google Play Games is optional — the game is fully playable without it. If you sign in, your Play Games display name and score may be visible to other players on the public leaderboard. Whether other players can see your name is controlled by your Play Games profile visibility setting, which you manage in the Google Play Games app, not by us. Your leaderboard score is stored by Google under your Google account and is not deleted when you uninstall our game.

7. Children’s privacy

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Data retention

Data stored on your device remains until you delete the app data or uninstall the game. Data held by the third parties listed above is retained according to their own policies. Leaderboard scores are retained by Google Play Games under your Google account.

9. International transfers

The third-party services we use operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including the United States. Those providers use safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required by law.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, to object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Residents of the EEA/UK have these rights under the GDPR; residents of California have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know and the right to delete, and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Because we do not hold an account for you, most requests are handled fastest directly with the relevant provider (Google, Unity or ironSource). You can also uninstall the game to remove all locally stored data. To make a request to us, use the contact details below.

11. Security

We rely on the security of the platforms we build on — Google Play, Google Play Games and the operating system on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example if we add a new feature or service. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version. Significant changes will be highlighted in the app or on this page.

13. How to delete your data

This section applies to STAX (Google Play package com.codeibex.stax), published by Coding Ibex. You can delete your data yourself using the steps below, or ask us to do it for you.

Option 1 — delete it yourself

  • Everything stored in the game: uninstall STAX from your device. This immediately and permanently deletes your high score, local score history, remaining lives and refill timer, sound/haptics settings, equipped skin, and the tutorial-seen flag. Nothing is kept, because none of it ever leaves your device.
  • Your leaderboard score: open the Google Play Games app → profile icon → Settings → Delete Play Games account data, where you can delete your data for an individual game or for Play Games entirely. Leaderboard scores are stored by Google under your Google account, so only you (or Google) can remove them.
  • Advertising data: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads, where you can delete or reset your advertising ID and opt out of ad personalisation. You can also request deletion directly from our advertising partners using the links in section 3.

Option 2 — ask us to delete it

Email Usman@codingibex.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. Please include the name of the app (STAX) and, if you signed in, your Google Play Games display name so we can identify the right record. We aim to respond within 30 days.
